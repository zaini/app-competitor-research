# Report outline

One HTML file, `<app>-market-research.html`, with a sticky left table of contents grouped as below. Aim for depth: a thorough report runs 30–60 screens of scrolling, with real data in every section.

## Start here
1. **Hero**: title, date, sources, 4 KPI tiles (e.g. the app's real MRR if known, the main rival's revenue*, the fastest riser, the most important keyword gap).
2. **The short version**: 1 paragraph on the market and the opportunity, then **10–15 numbered actions in priority order**, each with the evidence and a link to its section.
3. **Where the app stands today**: AppKittie screenshot(s), a table comparing AppKittie's estimates with real numbers (if given), current store listing, current ads as AppKittie sees them, current keyword positions.

## Competitors
4. **Market map**: one table of all competitors (icon, name, developer, store, downloads*, revenue*, 30-day revenue growth, rating, price points, paid ads seen, links). Separate tables for direct rivals and big generalists. Then 3–5 bullets on what the map says.
5. **Deep dive: main competitor**: dashboard screenshots, how they make money, ads and channels (including Meta Ad Library counts), onboarding strip, review analysis (complaint themes with counts), and "how to beat them" as a numbered plan.
6. **Deep dives: fastest riser / strategic threat / top advertiser** (as relevant).
7. **Other direct competitors**: one card each (numbers, 300-day sparkline if available, price points, how they pitch it, review summary, store screenshot strip, what they do well, what to borrow, links).
8. **Big generalists**: short cards focused on lessons (pricing, onboarding, ad formulas).
9. **Case studies the user asked about** (e.g. "why does app X earn more than me?").

## Marketing
10. **Organic and creators**: creator table (handle, platform, views, apps tagged), top-video gallery with covers and hooks, and a creator plan in tiers.
11. **Paid ads: what competitors run**: who advertises (table with evidence and main message), creative galleries (deduplicated, at most ~4 per app), the hook formulas that recur, and 6–8 creative ideas for the user. Links to the Meta Ad Library, the TikTok Creative Center and AppKittie's ads explorer.
12. **How much competitors spend on ads**: the estimate table and method box from `estimation-methods.md`, then "what this means for you".

## Stores and search
13. **App Store optimisation**: current metadata; keyword map (popularity, difficulty, current rank, what to do); proposed title / subtitle / keyword field as A/B variants; screenshot recommendations with side-by-side strips of the app vs the main rivals; localisation (other markets' keyword tables); AppKittie keyword ideas; custom product pages.
14. **Google Play optimisation** (if relevant): ranks, keyword table, short and long description advice, store listing experiments, ratings.
15. **Apple Search Ads plan**: campaign / ad group table (keywords, countries, starting max CPT, why), negatives, custom product page pairing, weekly rules.

## Product
16. **Pricing and paywall benchmarks**: price points for every competitor, patterns (weekly plans, annual anchors, tiers, lifetime, trials), and tests to run.
17. **Onboarding teardowns**: screen strips for 3–7 competitors with a one-line summary each, then ideas to A/B test.
18. **What users say**: a review theme table (reviews analysed, average, 1–2★ count, theme counts) and what users complain about vs praise.
19. **Feature ideas from competitors**: table of feature, seen in, priority (P0/P1/P2), notes for this app.

## Context
20. **Charts**: relevant top-grossing / top-free chart positions.
21. **Weekly playbook**: what to check in AppKittie each week, with links (Your Apps, favourites folder, ads, organic, Rising, Keyword Explorer, Meta Ad Library).
22. **Method, sources and caveats**: every source, what was estimated, what failed or is missing, and exactly what was changed in the user's AppKittie account.

## Style
- Plain sentence-case English, short paragraphs, real numbers. Mark estimates with `*`.
- Tables scroll horizontally on small screens; images lazy-load; dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Every competitor mention links to its AppKittie profile at least once.
- Don't paste long review text; summarise and quote at most a short phrase.
